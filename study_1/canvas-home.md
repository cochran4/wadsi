## **MATH 331: Introductory Probability**

**University of Wisconsin–Madison · Fall 2026**

Welcome to Math 331! This course develops the foundations of probability through mathematical models, problem solving, homework, and in-class quizzes. Use this page as a starting point for course information and resources.

### **At a Glance**

- **Lectures:** MWF in 6104 Sewell Social Sciences
- **Homework:** Usually due Thursdays at 11:59 p.m.
- **In-class quizzes:** Usually Fridays following homework deadlines
- **Instructors:** [Benedek Valkó](mailto:valko@math.wisc.edu) and [Amy Cochran](mailto:cochran4@wisc.edu)
